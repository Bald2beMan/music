I started my music creation journey back in 2020. Specificaly on 20201008 for the first time I exported my own music file called 'its_done.mp3'. From there i made plain unlistenable stuff until 2022, where I started experimentiation with using my own voice for melodies and other elements. Then I continued making garbage (with a few outliers like Addiction Clown) up until 2023. That year I made my first EP as a month long challange with my friend. The songs in there are at least listenable. While creating 1/2 Middles I grew a lot compared to 2020/22. In 2024 i entered a year of stagnation. I felt like I completely stopped experimenting with my music and every single song was pretty much the same. That year i released Listen and you'll hear - my first full lenght album. After procrastination filled production of that piece, I started shitting out generic mess. A few ouliers here and there, you can check them on the 2024 page. Thus the year 2025 started with me having zero motivation to do beats. The feeling of not improving didnt disappear. Now in october after making Discovering Balls I am feeling a hope that my journey won't end yet!
